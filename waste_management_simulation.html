Sustainable Development Goals

EcoSort AI directly supports SDG 11 (Sustainable Cities and Communities) and SDG 12 (Responsible Consumption and Production) by automating waste classification, reducing contamination, and enabling data-driven recycling decisions.

Computer Vision AI

Our AI model is trained exclusively on waste images covering all 9 major waste categories. It uses image feature extraction to classify waste with high accuracy without relying on any non-waste training data.

Virtual Sensor Network

Simulated IoT sensors measure weight, moisture content, electrical conductivity, and toxicity levels. These values feed into the decision engine to confirm and refine the AI's classification result.

Decision Engine

A rule-based engine combines AI output and sensor readings to determine which bin should open. It accounts for bin capacity, hazard levels, and biodegradability to make safe routing decisions.

Smart Bin System

9 bins correspond to each waste category. The correct bin lid opens automatically upon classification, with LED indicators and audio confirmation. Full bins trigger municipality alerts.

9 Waste Categories Supported

🍳
Cooking Waste
Food scraps, oils, kitchen organic matter. Biodegradable, non-hazardous.
🧴
Plastic Waste
Bottles, packaging, bags. Non-biodegradable, recyclable.
⚙️
Metal Waste
Cans, wires, scrap. Non-biodegradable, high recyclability value.
💻
E-Waste
Electronics, batteries, cables. Contains hazardous materials, needs special handling.
🌿
Bio-degradable Waste
Leaves, paper, natural fibers. Can be composted naturally.
🚫
Non-Biodegradable Waste
Styrofoam, certain plastics. Persists in environment, needs recycling.
🏥
Medical Waste
Syringes, bandages, expired meds. Requires special biohazard disposal.
⚠️
Biodegradable Hazardous
Pesticides, certain organic chemicals. Biodegrades but is toxic.
☣️
Non-Biodegradable Hazardous
Industrial chemicals, heavy metals. Most dangerous — strict containment needed.
